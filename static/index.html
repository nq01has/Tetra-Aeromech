Simultaneous 5-Axis Machining Centers

Our simultaneous 5-axis vertical machining centers (VMC) provide the structural dynamic rigidity required to machine complex contours, multi-bladed blisks, turbine impellers, and monolithic aerospace structural bulkheads in single setups—eliminating multiple setups and tolerance stack-up errors.

  • High-Speed Spindles up to 20,000 RPM with Direct Drive
  • Work Envelope up to 1000 mm × 800 mm × 600 mm
  • Linear Glass Scales with 0.0001 mm Resolution
  • Laser Tool Measurement & Automated In-Process Probing (Renishaw)

High-Precision Multi-Axis CNC Turning & Turn-Mill

Multi-spindle, multi-turret CNC turning centers equipped with driven live tooling, C-axis and Y-axis capability. Designed for cylindrical parts requiring sub-micron roundness, concentricity, and fine thread tolerances.

  • Twin Spindle & Dual Turret with Synchronized Part Transfer
  • Bar Capacity up to Ø65 mm; Chucking Capacity up to Ø320 mm
  • Deep-Hole Gun Drilling & Reaming down to 1 µm diametral control
  • Live Tooling up to 12,000 RPM for Milling, Tapping & Cross-Drilling

Advanced Aerospace Alloys & Superalloys

High-temperature superalloys and refractory metals demand specialized cutting tool geometries, rigid clamping, high-pressure through-spindle coolant (70 bar), and precise feed-rate regulation. We machine certified raw billets directly from approved mill sources.

  • Nickel Superalloys: Inconel 718 (AMS 5662), Inconel 625, Hastelloy X, Nimonic 90
  • Titanium Alloys: Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 (AMS 4928), Grade 23 (ELI), Beta-C
  • Aerospace Aluminum: 7075-T651, 2024-T351, 6061-T6, Al-Li
  • Precipitation Hardening Steels: 15-5 PH (AMS 5659), 17-4 PH, Maraging 300
